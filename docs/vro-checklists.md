# VRO registration checklists (USAG Stuttgart)

Transcribed **exactly** from the official USAG Stuttgart Vehicle
Registration Office PDFs Terry downloaded (2026-09-04) from
home.army.mil/stuttgart/my-garrison/all-services/vehicle-registration.
These are regulatory documents — do not paraphrase in customer-facing copy;
quote or link. Stuttgart-specific; other garrisons (Ramstein/KMC,
Wiesbaden, etc.) will have their own — confirm per location before launch.

Standing rule on all of them: *"IAW AEA 190-1 3-5 REGISTRATION
REQUIREMENTS: Individuals will not be authorized to conduct any vehicle
registration transactions until all of their existing registrations are in
compliance with this publication."*

---

## Buying: Used US-Spec Vehicle Purchased From Local Dealer

- Orders
  - Active Duty / Civilian — PCS Orders
  - Contractor — 1172
- Valid DoD ID card *(PDF says "DoW" — transcription artifact for DoD)*
- Valid USAREUR-AF Certificate of License
- Valid MSF Card (Active Duty Motorcycle Only)
- SOFA Card (Contractor and their dependents ONLY)
  - ID and SOFA Card — dates must match 1172
- Proof of Insurance for Germany — Blank ICC. USAA and Mirascon transmit
  digital cards and must be contacted at least 24 hours prior to
  appointment. All other local companies must provide a Deckungskarte
  (double white card). Policy holder will be listed as an owner of the
  vehicle and must be present to register.
- Bill of Sale
- **US Customs Form 550-175A**
- Most recent issued title:
  - Original USAREUR-AF Transfer Title, **or**
  - Original US Title if imported and not yet registered. Copy only
    accepted if financed.
- POV limit waiver (AE Form 190-1AG) approved by Commander (O3/GS
  Equivalent) if applicable
- Credit/Debit Card, Check or Money order for registration payment — $45

## Buying: Used Euro-Spec Vehicle Purchased From Dealer or Local National

- Orders
  - Active Duty / Civilian — PCS Orders
  - Contractor — 1172
- Valid DoD ID card
- Valid USAREUR-AF Certificate of License
- Valid MSF Card (Active Duty Motorcycle Only)
- SOFA Card (Contractor and their dependents ONLY)
  - ID and SOFA Card — dates must match 1172
- Proof of Insurance for Germany — Blank ICC. USAA and Mirascon transmit
  digital cards and must be contacted at least 24 hours prior to
  appointment. All other local companies must provide a Deckungskarte
  (double white card). Policy holder will be listed as an owner of the
  vehicle and must be present to register.
- Bill of Sale
  - Private Individual: must list Make, Model, Year and VIN plus the
    purchase price; buyer and seller info — names, addresses, signatures
    of both parties.
  - Dealership: **Kaufvertrag or Rechnung**. Must list Make, Model, Year
    and VIN. If VAT form is not used, must list taxes paid outside of sale
    price or denote use of **§ 25a UStG**. Must list all buyer info and
    business info — name, address, customs information if applicable.
- **VAT Form (if applicable)**
- **Original German title book (Fahrzeugbrief) / EU Title**
- **Original German registration (Fahrzeugschein) / EU Registration**
  - German Registration → Proof of deregistration required — must be
    official — MFR on official letterhead with signature and stamps.
    Emails or hand-written notes not acceptable.
  - **— OR —**
  - Original USAREUR-AF Transfer Title (if last registered in the
    USAREUR-AF System)
- POV limit waiver (AE Form 190-1AG) approved by Commander (O3/GS
  Equivalent or above)
- Credit/Debit Card, Check or Money order for registration payment — $45
  per year of registration. Max 2 years for used vehicles with valid
  inspection less than 30 days old from date of registration.

## Selling: To a Local National or Dealership (Sell It Back)

- Valid DoD ID card
- SOFA Card (Contractor and their Accompanied Dependent Only)
- License Plates
- Lien Release or Permission to Sell (dealership only) if applicable
- **Completed customs form 550-175B cleared through Zollamt**
- Bill of Sale

Notes:
- If a Joint / Co-owner is listed on the registration, both the Sponsor
  and Joint / Co-owner must sign the BOS, provide special POA to Seller,
  or provide a notarized bill of sale listing the person/company
  purchasing.
- All vehicles must be properly deregistered out of the USAREUR-AF System
  prior to clearing. If an owner can't dispose of / sell / ship a POV
  before departing, the unit commander assumes responsibility per AER
  190-1.
- Lost/Stolen license plates must be accompanied by an MP Report (AE Form
  190-1AU) and a $45 fee.

## Notes common to the purchase checklists

- **Sponsor must be present** regardless of purchaser listed on the BOS.
  Command Sponsored Spouses may register with a POA from the Sponsor.
- Service members on **Title 10 orders must have Orders for EVERY
  transaction.**
- A **BOS from a dealership/business must be official** and meet German
  tax-law requirements. Hand-written private BOS documents are **not**
  acceptable for purchases from a business.
- **German banks listed as lien holder must provide written authorization**
  that the vehicle will be registered in the USAREUR-AF System.
- A **USAREUR driver's license is required** to register a vehicle.
- All shipped vehicles and used vehicles purchased in Germany **must pass a
  safety inspection** before they can be registered.

## Who provides what — UCG vs. the customer (working split)

**UCG produces / handles:**
- Bill of Sale — official **Kaufvertrag or Rechnung** (German tax-law
  compliant; handwritten not acceptable for a dealer sale)
- VAT form (EU-spec path) and getting it stamped
- **US Customs Form 550-175A** (buy) / **550-175B** cleared through Zollamt
  (sell-back)
- Safety inspection / TÜV (**UCG pays**)
- German title book + registration + the official deregistration MFR
  (EU-spec), or the USAREUR-AF Transfer Title
- Lien release, if UCG holds one
- The **VRO cover letter** assembling all of the above in checklist order

**Customer brings themselves:**
- Orders (PCS Orders / 1172) — *this is why "Orders" is a document in the
  app's intake*
- DoD ID card, USAREUR-AF Certificate of License, SOFA card (if
  contractor), MSF card (if AD motorcycle)
- **Proof of Insurance for Germany** — eVB / Blank ICC / Deckungskarte,
  arranged with their insurer; **the policy holder is listed as a vehicle
  owner and must be present at the VRO**
- $45 (× up to 2 years) — card/check/money order
- POV limit waiver (AE Form 190-1AG) from their command, if they're over
  the POV limit
- German-bank lienholder authorization letter, if financed through a
  German bank

# Impressum template — needs real business details, not for publication as-is

**Not legal advice.** This is a structural template of what § 5 DDG
requires (see `docs/legal-considerations-germany.md` §2) — every
bracketed field below needs UCG's actual, verified business information,
and the finished result should get a quick review by counsel before
publishing, since getting this wrong is specifically the kind of thing
that draws formal warnings in Germany. Do not fill in placeholder or
guessed values and publish this — every field must be real and correct.

Where this needs to live once real: linked from the app (e.g., an
"Impressum" item on the Account tab) and from usedcarguys.net.

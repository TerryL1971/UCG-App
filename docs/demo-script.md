# Demo script — owners & management

**Goal:** show how a customer goes from the $300 deposit to driving away,
then selling the car back to UCG (or selling any car to UCG). About 15
minutes. Everything runs on sample data in **demo mode** — nothing is sent
to anyone.

## Before the meeting (10 minutes)

1. **Use your iPhone, mirrored to the screen** (the simulator has no camera
   or WhatsApp). Mac: plug in the iPhone → QuickTime Player → File → New
   Movie Recording → click the arrow next to the record button → choose
   your iPhone. Or AirPlay to a TV.
2. Start the app: `npx expo start` on the Mac, open it in Expo Go.
3. **Account:** Demo Mode **on** → tap **Reset Demo Data**.
4. **Account → Where are you stationed?** → **Stuttgart** (shows the
   Zollamt detail; other bases say "your salesperson confirms…").
5. Go once to **My Deal** and tap **Allow** on the notifications question,
   so it doesn't pop up mid-demo.
6. Have a sample PDF in **Files** for the "Add a PDF" moment: Documents →
   **Send all my documents** on the demo Audi → **Save to Files**. (It's a
   made-up demo document, safe to show.)
7. Turn on Do Not Disturb.

In demo mode every WhatsApp button shows **"Demo: WhatsApp to …"** with
the message it would send, instead of opening WhatsApp. Tap **OK**.

## Part 0 — Before the deposit (2 min, optional)

**Say:** *"Most of our conversations happen before the customer even lands —
like a family still in the US asking what they can get. The app starts
there too, but anyone who's already paid the $300 goes straight to their
plan."*

1. **Tools → Get Ready:** arrival date, base, wish list (automatic, around
   $350/month, small SUV, room for a car seat), how they'll pay, which
   location. **Save & Send My Wish List** → demo WhatsApp preview. *"It's
   saved in the app — WhatsApp's disappearing messages can't lose it."*
2. **Tools → Good to Know:** the questions every customer asks — dollars,
   no taxes, the 5-day deposit hold, deposits refundable and transferable,
   financing, moving a car between locations.
3. On a new account, My Deal shows both doors: **"Already put down your
   $300 deposit? Start My Car Plan"** and **"Still shopping? Get Ready"**.

## Part 1 — Buying: deposit to delivery (8 min)

**Say:** *"The salesperson sells the car and takes the $300 deposit, as
today. From that moment the customer uses the app. Nobody at UCG has to
log in or type anything — salespeople keep using DealerTeam and WhatsApp."*

1. **Create Account** (just show the screen): name, email, **WhatsApp
   number** and **base** are required. *"Every base registers cars
   differently, so the plan follows their base."*
2. **My Deal → "Buying or selling another car? Add it"** →
   **Start My Car Plan** → tap **Demo: fill in a sample Purchase Order** →
   **Build My Plan**.
   *"The customer copies a few things off the Purchase Order the
   salesperson gave them. Later the app can read the PDF by itself."*
3. **Step 2 – Car Being Prepared.** Tick **"My salesperson says my car is
   Retail Ready"** → a banner appears: **pay $31,199.00 by [date]**.
   *"5-day window, worked out automatically. They get reminders."*
   Point at **Your UCG team**: the salesperson, one tap to WhatsApp, and
   (once UCG's list is in) the registration and logistics contacts and
   the showroom hours.
4. **Step 3 – Pay the Balance → How to Pay.** Show the Wise details, copy
   buttons, the **wire-fraud warning**. *"Bank details only ever come from
   the app."*
5. Point at the **American Auto Nation card**: *"The moment they pay, we
   push our own insurance — first month on us."* Tap **Get My American
   Auto Nation Quote** → the demo WhatsApp preview shows the message to the
   salesperson.
6. Back on the step: tick **"UCG has confirmed they received my payment."**
7. **Step 4 – Insure Your Car:** tick **"My car is insured."** (Show it
   can be undone — *"if they switch from USAA to AAN later"*.)
8. **Step 5 – Base Customs Office:** read the instructions (5 Purchase
   Order copies, 3–4 back is normal). *"They scan the 550 at the counter."*
   Optionally tap **Scan AE Form 550-175A** and photograph any paper.
9. **Step 6 – UCG Clears Customs:** shows the Stuttgart Zollamt note. Tick
   **"UCG has cleared customs — my registration packet is ready."**
10. **Step 7 – Registration & Plates:** **What to Bring to the VRO**
    (checklist for their base). *"They scan their TT Doc when they have
    it."* Use **→** to go to step 8.
11. **Step 8 – Pick Up Your Car:** **Take My Pickup Photo** (hand the phone
    to someone: *"the salesperson takes their photo with the car"*), then
    **Leave UCG a Google Review** (opens UCG Ramstein's Google page). Tick
    **"I've picked up my car"** → banner: **inspection guarantee ends
    [date]**, 5 business days.
12. **Documents tab:** every paper for the car — **Add** on Purchase Order
    → **Add a PDF** → pick the sample from Files. Show **Send all my
    documents** (one PDF for WhatsApp). *"Everything that goes with the car
    stays in their pocket for as long as they own it."*
13. **Tools tab:** quick tour — Key Dates (registration and license expiry
    reminders), Currency Converter, American Auto Nation, Service Center.

*(Optional: the demo **Opel** shows a DEN car — cashier's check for the
Total Price, VAT office, never a deposit.)*

## Part 2 — Selling back to UCG (4 min)

**Say:** *"Years later they PCS. The same app gets their car back to us."*

1. **Sell My Car tab →** pick the **BMW** they just bought → the form fills
   itself in (VIN, year, spec, name, base). Add today's mileage and a photo.
2. **Send My Offer Request** → demo WhatsApp preview to the salesperson.
3. **Send My Car's Documents** → one PDF of all its paperwork — *"the
   buying team gets the payoff paperwork straight away"*.
4. **I've Accepted My Offer** → enter an amount → **Book a Pre-Buy
   Inspection**.
5. **My Deal → Add → Start My Car Plan → "Selling my car to UCG"** → **Demo:
   fill in a sample Bill of Sale** → **Build My Plan**. Walk the selling
   steps: 550-175B at customs (valid 90 days — tracked), UCG clears
   customs, hand over car/keys/title, deregister, **UCG pays within 30 days
   of deregistration** (date shown).

**Any car, not bought from UCG:** Sell My Car → **A different car** →
type the VIN or **Scan** it — same flow.

## If someone asks…

- **"Does a salesperson have to do anything in the app?"** No. They keep
  using DealerTeam and WhatsApp. The customer ticks what the salesperson
  tells them.
- **"What if the customer ticks something wrong?"** Every step can be
  undone, and there's "Change car or start over".
- **"Can it read the paperwork itself / check the VIN matches?"** Planned:
  reading the Purchase Order PDF and checking VIN, names and prices.
- **"When is it in the App Store?"** Needs UCG developer accounts (D-U-N-S
  number), the lawyer review, and a few weeks of testing — see
  `docs/pre-launch-checklist.md`.
- **"What does it cost to run?"** Supabase Pro ($25/month) once real
  customers use it; Apple $99/year; Google $25 once.

## After the demo
Account → **Reset Demo Data** puts all sample deals back.
